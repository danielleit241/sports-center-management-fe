# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
- Prospective members exploring the sports center's services and membership packages.
- Current members using the authenticated app to manage their participation, including class registration.
- Center managers using the authenticated app to manage operational catalogues such as membership packages.

## Product Purpose
The frontend is the public-facing entry point for a sports-center experience, with a separate authenticated application for members and center managers. It should help prospective and current members understand the available services and complete relevant tasks, while giving managers clear access to supported operational workflows.

## Brand Personality
Energetic and trustworthy. Communication should feel welcoming and motivating without overstating what the center or system provides.

## Anti-references
- Avoid high-pressure sales tactics, false scarcity, or unsupported health and performance claims.
- Avoid marketing language or visual decoration that obscures the next useful action.
- Avoid generic fitness clichés that make the center feel interchangeable.

## Design Principles
1. Earn trust with accurate, understandable information; do not imply capabilities or outcomes the system cannot support.
2. Make the transition between public discovery and authenticated tasks clear.
3. Help members and managers identify the next relevant action without unnecessary friction.
4. Keep operational feedback explicit: show loading, success, empty, and error states where they affect a task.
5. Treat accessibility as part of the interaction, not a finishing pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation and visible focus, screen-reader-friendly semantics and feedback, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and state communication that does not rely on color alone.
